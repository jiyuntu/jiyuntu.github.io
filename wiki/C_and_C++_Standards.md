The working/final drafts of the C and C++ standards are on http://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg14/ and http://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/. The rationale document could be found similarly, for example, [Draft C99 Rationale](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg14/www/docs/n897.pdf). The published standards could be found on the ISO website, for example, [C++23](https://www.iso.org/standard/83626.html). Defect reports could be [filed](https://isocpp.org/std/submit-issue) to the language standard.
